About & Help

About Audio Immersiver

Audio Immersiver is an easy-to-use music player with a spinning vinyl disc, 15-band equalizer, 3D surround sound, room echo, and custom color themes.

Add songs from your device or search free online music to listen your way.

Privacy

  • Your Music Stays on Your Device: Songs you add from your phone or computer, along with your playlists and sound settings, are saved in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.
  • Online Music Search: Online search is off until you turn it on. When you search or play online songs, your browser connects directly to Audius, Internet Archive, or Wikimedia Commons.
  • No Accounts or Tracking: No sign-up is needed, and there are no ads or tracking scripts.

Music Rights & Licenses

  • Respect Artists: Online songs come from independent artists and open archives (Creative Commons and Public Domain). Please respect the artist's license terms.
  • Allowed Downloads Only: Only download songs when the artist or license allows it.
  • Independent Player: Audio Immersiver is an independent project and is not affiliated with Audius, Internet Archive, or Wikimedia.

Online Music Notice

Before turning on online music search, please note:

  • Direct Connection: Searching or playing online music connects your browser directly to Audius, Internet Archive, or Wikimedia Commons.
  • Respect Artist Licenses: Only download or share songs when the artist or license allows it.
  • Safe & Open: This player only uses public, open-licensed music sources.
