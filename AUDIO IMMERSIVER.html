About & Help

About

Audio Immersiver is a personal music player with a 15-band equalizer, 3D spatial sound, room reverb, custom themes, and reactive vinyl visuals.

Playback smoothness depends on your device, browser, and network connection.

Privacy

  • Local Music Stays on Your Device: Imported audio files, playlists, equalizer settings, and themes are saved locally in your browser (IndexedDB and LocalStorage) and are never uploaded.
  • Online Streaming: Online search is off by default. When enabled, your browser connects directly to Audius, Internet Archive, or Wikimedia Commons under their privacy policies.
  • No Tracking: No accounts, ads, or analytics scripts are used.

Licenses

  • Creator Rights: Online tracks come from independent artists and open archives (Creative Commons and Public Domain). Please respect attribution and license terms.
  • Downloads: Only download tracks when permitted by the artist or license. Audio Immersiver never bypasses DRM or access controls.
  • Independent App: Not affiliated with Audius, Internet Archive, or Wikimedia Foundation.

Online Music Notice

Before using online search and streaming, please review these terms:

  • Direct Requests: Searching or streaming connects your browser directly to Audius, Internet Archive, or Wikimedia Commons under their privacy policies.
  • License Respect: Only download or reuse tracks when permitted by the track license or artist.
  • Authorized Streams: Streams use public APIs and never bypass DRM or paywalls.
