About & Privacy

About Audio Immersiver

Audio Immersiver is a personal music player designed to make listening calming, customizable, and engaging. Import your own music or stream from open-licensed libraries with a 15-band equalizer, spatial 3D sound, room reverb, and reactive visuals.

Playback smoothness depends on your device hardware, browser audio support, network connection, and stream availability.

Privacy Policy

  • Local Music Stays on Your Device: Audio files you import from your device, along with your playlists, equalizer settings, and themes, are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB and LocalStorage) and are not uploaded to any server.
  • Online Features & Third-Party Requests: Online search is off by default. When you enable Online Search or play an external stream, your browser connects directly to the selected provider (Audius, Internet Archive, or Wikimedia Commons). Those services receive standard network request details—such as your IP address and search query—under their own privacy policies.
  • No Accounts or App Tracking: Audio Immersiver does not require registration and does not include advertising or analytics scripts.

Copyright & Licensing Notice

  • Respect Creator Rights & Licenses: Online tracks come from independent artists and open-licensed archives (Creative Commons and Public Domain). Always check and follow the track's license terms, such as attribution or non-commercial rules.
  • Legitimate Downloads Only: Not all online music is free or permitted to download. Only download tracks when the provider offers a legitimate download and the applicable license or artist permission allows it. Audio Immersiver never bypasses DRM, paywalls, or access controls.
  • Independent Project: Audio Immersiver is not affiliated with or endorsed by Audius, the Internet Archive, or the Wikimedia Foundation.

Online Music Search & Copyright Notice

Before using online music search and streaming, please review and acknowledge these terms:

  • Third-Party Network Requests: Connecting to Audius, Internet Archive, or Wikimedia Commons sends standard network requests (including your IP address) directly from your browser to those providers under their own privacy policies.
  • License & Download Compliance: Only download or reuse tracks when the provider offers a legitimate download and the track's license or creator permission allows it. Do not assume all online streams are free to download or redistribute.
  • No Circumvention: This player streams only publicly available, authorized endpoints and never bypasses DRM, paywalls, or access restrictions.
