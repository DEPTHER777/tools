About & Privacy

About Audio Immersiver

Audio Immersiver was created by an independent developer who found music helpful during stressful moments and wanted to create a more immersive way to experience sound. The goal is to let people explore music through audio effects and a clean, personal listening experience.

Playback smoothness depends on your device hardware, browser audio scheduling, network conditions, and external stream availability.

Privacy & IP Protection

  • Local-First Processing: Imported audio files, 15-band equalization (with Wave-Curve Slider Linking and Auto Headroom Compensation), Sub-Bass harmonic boost, Air exciter, Stereo Widener, Warm Tube Saturation, 432Hz pitch tuning, 3D HRTF spatial panning, reverb impulse generation, and dynamics limiting run locally on your device.
  • Zero Personal Data Collection: This application does not create user accounts, tracking identifiers, analytics, telemetry, or advertising profiles. It does not collect or store your name, email address, contacts, search history, or online listening history.
  • IP Address & Network Honesty: This application never logs, displays, or stores your IP address. However, HTTPS encryption only protects data in transit and does not conceal your source IP address from external servers you connect to. When you enable online search or play external streams, the music provider (Audius, Internet Archive, or Wikimedia Commons) receives standard network connection details (including your IP address) under its own privacy policy. Using the optional companion backend proxy masks your client IP from upstream search APIs for proxied search requests, though direct media streams still connect from your browser.
  • Minimal Local Storage: Only your local imported library (in IndexedDB) and theme/audio slider values (in LocalStorage) stay on your device. Online search queries are never persisted.

Credits & Open Licensing Disclosures

  • Core Web Technologies: Built with standard HTML5 Media, CSS Neumorphic UI, IndexedDB, Canvas 2D, and the W3C Web Audio API (BiquadFilterNode, WaveShaperNode, DelayNode, PannerNode HRTF, ConvolverNode, DynamicsCompressorNode, and AnalyserNode).
  • Audius HQ: Full-length open music search and direct in-player streaming powered by the public Audius REST API for artist-authorized streams.

  • Wikimedia Commons Free Audio: Queries the official Wikimedia Commons MediaWiki API, where all hosted audio files are strictly Public Domain or Creative Commons licensed, displaying per-track license badges.
